Your library belongs to you.

BookSafe is a book manager for iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch: scan books by ISBN, track your reading, stay on top of series. What sets it apart from Goodreads and StoryGraph: no account, no tracking, no third-party servers. Data lives on the device and syncs only through the user’s own private iCloud. Built by a family in southern Germany.

Five key features

  • Add books in secondsScan an ISBN and title, author, cover and publisher arrive automatically. CSV import handles Goodreads exports.
  • From reading day to reading yearDaily page, reading sessions with a timer, statistics, a yearly challenge — and at year’s end, a year in review as a film.
  • Wishlist, countdown and library loansA widget counts down the days to release; borrowed books carry a countdown that reminds you before they are due back.
  • Start from your wristNew in 1.1: start a reading session on Apple Watch — even with no iPhone in range.
  • Series that finally add upVolume numbers from several library catalogues — special volumes such as “book 8.5” included.

Privacy — the facts

“Data Not Collected.”App Store privacy label

  • No user account, no sign-up, no email address.
  • No analytics SDK, no ad network, no third-party frameworks.
  • Sync through Apple’s CloudKit in the private iCloud — no developer access.
  • Outgoing connections only for book metadata — carrying the ISBN, never a user identifier.
  • Export any time as CSV or PDF.
  • No in-app review prompt.

A note for quoting: BookSafe is not “end-to-end encrypted”, and as of 1.0 it is no longer “fully offline” either. The accurate phrasing is: no third-party servers, only your own private iCloud, works offline.
