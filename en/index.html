Your library.
Beautiful, fast, private.

BookSafe is the book app with no account, no tracking, no subscription — for iPhone and iPad.

Private

Your library is yours

  • No account, no ads, no data collection — your library lives on your device and in your private iCloud, nowhere else.
  • iPhone + iPad, always in sync — no sign-up needed. Works offline, too; only the ISBN lookup queries public book databases.
  • Export your library as CSV or PDF anytime — your data is yours.

Today

Your reading day at a glance

  • The Today tab greets you with a daily page: your yearly goal, the book you’re currently reading and your upcoming dates.
  • A new, curated quote of the day — in a card design that changes daily.
  • Two columns on iPad — with “Coming Up” and “Recently Finished”.

Series

Series that finally add up

  • Scan an ISBN — title, author, cover, publisher and more are fetched automatically from several book databases.
  • Series are grouped automatically — see which volume you're missing and add it in the edition you prefer.
  • Special volumes like “book 8.5” are sorted correctly, too.

Fair pricing

Buy once, no subscription

  • Free for up to 50 books — with every feature.
  • BookSafe Pro: a one-time €3.99 purchase for unlimited books. No subscription.
  • No hidden costs, no ads — buy once, yours forever.

Show it off

Beautiful enough to share

  • Share your favourite books as beautiful share cards.
  • Statistics, reading streak, badges and a yearly reading challenge with your own goal.
  • On release day, BookSafe celebrates with a big cover and a little fireworks display — widgets for your Home and Lock Screen included.

Recap

Your reading year, as a film

  • At the end of the year BookSafe turns your finished books into a recap: books, pages, top genre, most productive month and longest book.
  • Crown your three favourites on the top-three podium — gold, silver, bronze; BookSafe suggests your highest-rated ones.
  • And if you like, as an animated film to watch and share.

Free for up to 50 books. BookSafe Pro: one-time €3.99, no subscription.

Every feature is already included in the free version — Pro simply removes the limit.

Who is behind BookSafe

A family in southern Germany. The father writes the code, the mother proofreads every line and finds the mistakes nobody else does, and their daughter collects book series — the fact that series detection gets even “book 8.5” right is down to her standards. The release-day fireworks were her idea, too.

There is no account and no tracking, and that was never a product decision — it was a family decision.
