What’s new in version 1.13

On the App Store since 22 November 2026, on Google Play since 21 November 2026

← Back to the home page

What this update is about

BookSafe 1.13 lets you start a book you’ve already read once more, and every read counts. It also adds a recommended age, sorting by date read, the “Candlelight” share design and the “Dark” mood.

The same version 1.13 has been on Google Play since 21 November 2026. Android has no widgets and no watch. You buy BookSafe Pro there once on Google Play; the purchase does not unlock the iPhone app.

This page describes the changes in more detail than fits into the App Store release notes.

Version 1.13 · on the App Store since 22 November 2026, on Google Play since 21 November 2026

New

1Rereading

Start a book you’ve read once more from “More actions”. It then shows as “Rereading”, and for audiobooks as “Relistening”. Every read counts towards your annual goal, Year in Review and stamps. The book’s details list every read with its date and your impression.

2Recommended age

Where the publisher provides one, the book’s details show it, for example “12+ years” — for books on your wishlist too. Library and wishlist show it in short form (“12+”), and a shared quick review shows it in full. You can change it or add your own at any time. To hide it in lists and when sharing, go to Settings › Appearance.

3Sort by date read

You can now sort your library by date read.

4“Candlelight” share design

For Advent there is the “Candlelight” share design for reading updates and quick reviews. Each candle burns down as far as you’ve read.

5“Dark” mood

Your impression gains the “Dark” mood.

Improved

  • Reading sessions now also show the page you read up to
  • You can leave the date read empty if you no longer remember when you finished a book
  • Search now tells you when there’s no connection, instead of reporting “No matches found”
  • If the German National Library isn’t responding, BookSafe now says so when you add a book by ISBN and in “Search book data again” too

Plus many further refinements.

Good to know

RequirementsiPhone and iPad on iOS 18 or later, Apple Watch on watchOS 11 or later. Android on Google Play, with no widgets and no watch
Your dataOn iPhone and iPad: no account, no tracking, sync through your own private iCloud. On Android the library stays on the device. Pro does not carry across platforms.
PriceFree for up to 50 books. BookSafe Pro: €3.99 one-time on the App Store or €3.99 billed via Google Play, in Germany; prices vary by country. No subscription. A purchase applies only on the platform where you bought it.
UpdatingOn iPhone through the App Store, on Android through Google Play.
